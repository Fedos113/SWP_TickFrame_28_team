## Limitations Encountered

- AI cannot attend or record meetings — the transcript was based on a verbatim text recording provided by the team
- AI cannot verify deployment status, run the application, or validate customer claims — all factual assertions (e.g., "PostgreSQL migration completed") are based on statements made during the review
- AI cannot assess code quality or architecture decisions first-hand — analysis reflects the team's self-reported progress
- Report structure and content rely on the accuracy and completeness of the input transcript
