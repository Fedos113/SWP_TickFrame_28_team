## Limitations Encountered

- AI cannot attend meetings or record transcripts — the sprint review transcript was based on a text recording provided by the team
- AI cannot verify factual claims — all assertions about feature status, customer feedback, and deployment state reflect the team's self-reported progress
- AI cannot generate images or screenshots — all visual evidence was captured manually
- AI-generated documentation requires human review to ensure accuracy, tone, and alignment with team-specific context
- Report content quality depends on the accuracy and completeness of the input information
